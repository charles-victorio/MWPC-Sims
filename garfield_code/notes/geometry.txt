in the center, approximate it as extending infinitely in the x and z directions

wire
- wire pitch - how far away the wires are from each other (in the x direction)
    - go w 3 mm
- fixed: wire diameter - 20 um
- how many wires - this just sets the effective detector area. you'd probably determine this from your desired detector area
- other: material is gold-plated tungsten. tungsten for conductivity (I think), gold-plated for corrosion resistance (explain)
- guard wires? field-shaping wires?

volume
- anode-cathode gap - the distance (in the z direction) between the anode wires and the cathode plates on the top and bottom. The mwpc has reflection symmetry about the xz plane ie the gap btwn the bottom plate and the wires is equal to the gap between the wires and the top plate
- effective detector area - how big of an area that the detector can catch muons
- how much margin - there should be space (in the x direction) between the chamber frame and the leftmost wire, and the rightmost wire and the chamber frame. The electric field could get messed up by microscopic irregularities on the inner wall of the chamber frame. give it some space so the electric field dies out before it reaches the wall. - 3 cm is probably fine

gas
- fixed: gas compounds: what compounds are in the gas - Argon and carbon dioxide
- fixed: gas ratio: the ratio of compounds in the gas - 75% argon 25% carbon dioxide
- fixed, low priority / confirm once and it'll be fine: gas purity - if the detector has even a very small amount of O2, it will ruin the gain

electrical
- anode voltage - how much (positive) voltage to have on the anode wires - 1-4kV
